Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how vBlueTech (“we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through this website, vbluetech.com.

Information we collect

Quote form. When you submit our Request a Quote form, we collect the information you provide: your name, business name, email address, phone number, business type, the services you select and your message.

Demo bookings. If you book a demo call, the booking is handled by Calendly. Calendly collects the details you enter (such as your name, email and chosen time) and shares them with us so we can hold the call. Calendly’s own privacy policy applies to its service.

Email. If you email us at hello@vbluetech.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Netlify, which also processes quote form submissions on our behalf. As part of providing hosting, Netlify may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, prepare quotes, schedule and hold demo calls, and communicate with you about our services.

No selling of data

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and respond to you (such as Netlify and Calendly).

Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to you and for our ordinary business records, and then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@vbluetech.com.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; the right to request deletion; the right to request correction of inaccurate information; the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it); and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email hello@vbluetech.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Cookies

We keep cookies to a minimum and do not use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider (Netlify) and our booking tool (Calendly) may set cookies or similar technologies that are needed for their services to work, such as security and spam protection on forms and booking pages.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@vbluetech.com.
