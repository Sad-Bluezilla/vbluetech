FAQ

Common questions

How long does setup usually take?

Typically a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on scope. Your final timeline is confirmed in your quote.

What happens if the AI makes a mistake?

It isn’t perfect, so we plan for that. We test before launch, set it to transfer the call or take a message when it’s unsure, and review conversations and refine it after go-live.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. Usually you forward your existing number to the AI receptionist, either for all calls or only after hours.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your business hours, services, common questions, booking and call-transfer details, and brand information. We collect this through a short onboarding questionnaire.

Is there a contract? How do quotes work?

There are no fixed packages. After a demo call we send a custom quote, and we sign a written agreement before any work starts.
